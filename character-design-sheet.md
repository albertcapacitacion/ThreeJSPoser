# Character design sheet

## Purpose
Develop a consistent character reference that can be combined with
a ThreeJSPoser pose export to generate detailed 2D illustrations.

## Starting input
The user may provide:
- Character images.
- A character name and its specific version or outfit.
- A written character concept.
- Any combination of these.

Identify the supplied references and their roles.
Ask about consequential missing details before making design decisions.
Mark details as confirmed, uncertain or proposed.
Keep unavailable details explicitly unknown.

## Anatomy and proportions
Describe visible features with enough precision to reproduce them:
- Overall silhouette and build.
- Head-to-body proportions.
- Shoulder, chest, waist and hip relationships.
- Torso and limb lengths.
- Muscle definition and body contours.
- Hand, finger, foot and toe characteristics.
- Nonhuman anatomy, where applicable.

Identify proportions as observed or estimated.
Do not invent exact measurements from an image.

## Head and face
- Head shape, jaw and chin.
- Eyes, eyebrows, nose, mouth and ears.
- Skin tone, markings, scars or other distinctive features.
- Hair shape, hairline, length, color and arrangement.
- Facial hair, accessories and headwear.
- Features that identify the character across different angles.

## Clothing, equipment and materials
For each item, describe:
- Shape, fit, length and layering.
- Colors and visible materials.
- Seams, closures, trim, patterns and symbols.
- Attachment points and placement.
- Interaction with the body during movement.

Record asymmetric details using the character's anatomical left/right.
Identify hidden or unresolved construction details.

## Visual reference requirements
Select and explain the views needed for this character:
- Front, side and back full-body views.
- Additional side view when asymmetry requires it.
- Three-quarter view when it clarifies forms.
- Face and hairstyle close-ups.
- Hands, footwear, costume or equipment details as needed.

Confirm reference pose, expression, background and layout.
All views must show the same approved design.
Label views and anatomical sides clearly.

## Combining this design with a pose export
- Character references establish identity, proportions and appearance.
- Pose references establish gesture, joint arrangement and limb overlap.
- The chosen output camera establishes composition.
- Adapt the pose to the character's proportions.
- Translate mannequin segments into the character's anatomy.
- Resolve conflicting references explicitly.

Obtain expression, eye direction, support/contact, prop interactions,
framing and aspect ratio from the illustration brief.

## Deliverables
When asked to develop a character:
1. Complete this Markdown specification.
2. List unresolved questions and proposed details requiring approval.
3. Prepare generation prompts for the necessary reference views.
4. Generate the visual design sheet when requested.
5. Prepare a concise instruction for combining the approved character
   references with a pose export.

Check consistency of face, proportions, colors, outfit, accessories
and asymmetric details across the resulting views.
